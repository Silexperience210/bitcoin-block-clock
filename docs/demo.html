What you are watching

  • Block strip — the pending block fills like a liquid (waves, bubbles); the last four blocks are coloured by median fee, and a mined block slides out of the mempool into the chain.
  • Pools war — the 7-day mining pool race, with medals, shine and count-up.
  • Honest numbers — the block timer is memoryless (elapsed time, rarity, median, 6-confirmation ETA), fee levels get their own ETA, and direction odds are shown with independent samples and a 95 % interval — “no measurable edge” when it includes 50 %.
  • Volatility cone — calibrated 50 %/80 % cone on the 7D/30D chart, with its own on-device track record.
