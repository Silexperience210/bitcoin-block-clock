## 3. `firmware/bitcoin-block-clock/smooth_font.h` — digit glyphs

- **Generated by:** `firmware/tools/gen_smooth_font.py` (Pillow)
- **Source font:** **Arial Bold** (`C:/Windows/Fonts/arialbd.ttf`)

Arial is a proprietary Monotype font: its outlines may not be redistributed,
and a rasterized bitmap glyph set is a derived work. The 18 glyphs currently
committed are therefore **excluded from the Apache-2.0 grant**.

Fix (recommended, no visual redesign needed): regenerate the file from an
open-licensed font — e.g. DejaVu Sans Bold (free), Inter or Roboto (OFL),
or Liberation Sans Bold (SIL OFL, metric-compatible with Arial) — by pointing
`FONT_PATH` at the new `.ttf` and re-running the generator.
